# Portility — Chrome Web Store Listing (v1.7.4, V2 positioning)

## Name
Portility

## Summary (132 chars max)
The usability layer above Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini — move conversations, get a second opinion, and carry your context anywhere.

## Description
Today's AI platforms don't talk to each other. Switch tools and you lose your thread, your instructions, and your context. Portility sits on top of Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini so none of that gets lost — a floating button lives right next to the chat box, ready whenever you need it, on Windows or Mac.

SUPPORTED PLATFORMS
• Claude (claude.ai)
• ChatGPT (chatgpt.com)
• Gemini (gemini.google.com)

KEY FEATURES

Right where you're already typing
A floating Portility button sits next to the message box on every supported platform — no digging through toolbars. One click opens Port My Chat, Port My Profile, or Second Opinion, right from the conversation you're in. Built and tuned for both Windows and Mac, so the button lands in the right spot and sign-in works the same way regardless of where you are.

Port My Chat
Export any conversation from one AI platform and continue it seamlessly on another. Copy the full conversation, get an AI-generated summary, or save it as a .txt file — then pick up right where you left off with full context intact. Starting fresh? Build a new instruction profile in the same flow, without leaving the page.

Second Opinion
Get an instant second take on any AI response. Portility sends the conversation to another AI model and shows a side-by-side comparison — a real agreement gauge broken into factual accuracy, conclusion alignment, and reasoning consistency, plus verbatim quotes behind every point of agreement or divergence. Print it or download it as Markdown to keep for later.

Port My Profile
Create custom instruction profiles — for work, home, hobbies, and more — and carry them across platforms. Define how you like to work once, and every AI you use will know it.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Visit any supported AI platform
2. Click the floating Portility button next to the chat box, or the toolbar icon
3. Choose "Port My Chat" to export, "Second Opinion" to compare, or manage your profiles from Settings
4. Portility handles the formatting and transfer automatically

PRIVACY & SECURITY
• Conversation content is processed to power features like Second Opinion and AI summaries, but is not retained by Portility after processing
• Second Opinion results are cached locally in your browser for a short time so you can review them
• Google Sign-In is used for authentication; saved profile data is encrypted
• No conversation data is sold or shared with third parties

Portility is currently free to use during our beta — every feature, including Second Opinion and custom Profiles, is unlocked for signed-in users. Visit https://portility.ai for more information.
